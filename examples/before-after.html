Decelerate Smoothly on Entrances

Entering elements should arrive with initial speed and brake to a halt. Linear or accelerating entrances slam awkwardly into place, jarring peripheral vision.

Principle 04

Scale Buttons to 0.97 on Press

Interactive buttons should acknowledge clicks physically. A slight inward depression provides instant tactile confirmation that the action registered.

Flawed scale(1.0) · Dead Click
Feels stiff and unyielding like frozen plastic
Polished scale(0.97) translateY(1px)
Mechanical depression confirms actuation
Principle 05

Stretch Moving Knobs by 10–15%

Rapidly moving elements appear more organic when they deform slightly in the direction of travel, preserving volume before snapping back to rest.

Flawed Rigid Circle (No stretch)
Stiff geometric circle slides without personality
Polished scale(1.24, 0.82) stretch
Volume squashes and stretches with momentum

Principle 07

Follow Natural Curved Arcs for Diagonal Motion

Physical objects rarely travel in mathematical diagonal lines. Decoupling horizontal and vertical curves creates organic parabolic motion.

Principle 08

Confirm Successful Actions with an Outward Pulse

Changing button text quietly is easily missed in peripheral vision. An expanding ring or subtle scale bounce provides unmistakable feedback.

Principle 09

Use Ease-in-out for Continuous On-Screen Travel

When an element is already on-screen and shifts between states, it must both accelerate from rest and decelerate to a stop.

Flawed Linear (Constant Speed)

Polished cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)
Natural acceleration and gentle braking

Principle 13

Avoid Bouncy Springs in Everyday Work UI

Underdamped bouncy springs wobble repeatedly. In productivity interfaces, notifications must settle decisively with critical damping.

Principle 14

1:1 Direct Pointer Tracking During Drag

When dragging cards or controls, track the pointer coordinates directly with zero latency. Elastic delay makes controls feel laggy and sloppy.

Principle 15

Principle 18

Crossfade Icons with Optical Scale & Blur

Hard-cutting between icons looks glitchy. Combining scale reduction (down to 0.5) and a slight 4px blur makes distinct SVG geometries morph naturally.

Flawed Instant Hard Cut
Hard cut feels like a broken asset flicker
Polished Scale 0.5 + 4px Blur Morph
Silky optical morph looks deliberately designed
